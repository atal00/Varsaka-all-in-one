Most QA vendors won't put a number on their site. We think that's a mistake — so here's a straight answer.

## The three pricing models

QA outsourcing almost always falls into one of three shapes:

- **Fixed-price cycles** — a defined test pass per release. Predictable, scoped, billed per cycle. Typically starts around **$1,500 per cycle** for a focused web app.
- **Monthly retainer** — a block of senior QA capacity each month. Best for teams shipping continuously; commonly **$2,500–$8,000/month** depending on coverage.
- **Time & materials** — for hard-to-scope work like a VAPT engagement or a performance investigation. Estimated up front after a scoping call.

## What actually moves the number

The range above is wide because the price tracks four things:

1. **Scope of coverage** — one critical flow vs the whole product.
2. **Your stack** — a standard React + REST app is cheaper to cover than a sprawling microservice estate.
3. **What already exists** — building an automation framework from scratch costs more than extending one.
4. **Turnaround** — compressed, pre-launch timelines cost more than steady cadence.

## What you should budget

For an early-stage SaaS getting serious about QA, a realistic first-year budget is a **fixed-price cycle per release** while you stabilize, moving to a **retainer** once you're shipping weekly. The cheapest QA is the bug you never ship — the math almost always favors testing earlier.
