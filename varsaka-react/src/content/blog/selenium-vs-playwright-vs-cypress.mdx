There's no universally "best" framework — only the right fit for your stack and team. Here's how the three compare where it matters.

## The short version

- **Playwright** — our default for new suites. Fast, parallel by design, excellent auto-waiting, real cross-browser (Chromium, Firefox, WebKit), great debugging (trace viewer). Microsoft-backed and shipping fast.
- **Cypress** — best developer experience for teams already in it. Superb time-travel debugging and a friendly API. Weaker on multi-tab/multi-origin flows and true cross-browser breadth.
- **Selenium** — the veteran. Unmatched language and browser-grid support, huge ecosystem. More boilerplate, slower to write, and you assemble the waiting/reporting yourself.

## How to choose

| If you… | Pick |
|---|---|
| Are starting fresh and want speed + cross-browser | Playwright |
| Already use Cypress and your flows are single-origin | Cypress |
| Need broad language support or a large legacy grid | Selenium |

## What rarely changes the outcome

Framework choice matters less than **suite design**. A well-structured Cypress suite beats a tangled Playwright one every time. Stable locators, isolated tests, and CI integration drive reliability far more than the logo on the tin.
